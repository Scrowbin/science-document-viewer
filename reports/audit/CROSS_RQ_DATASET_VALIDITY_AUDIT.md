## 0. Decision summary

**Overall verdict:** the datasets are honest and traceable at undergraduate scope.
- Every cohort used by RQ1–RQ5 was recovered by ID.
- Every headline number checked reproduces.
- No hidden overlap was found: RQ5 and RQ4 Test do not overlap the earlier experiments.

The main problems are wording and method descriptions, not data. One group of RQ4 claims is contradicted by evidence already on disk.

**Defensible as written, or with small qualifications:**
- RQ1:
  - `pymupdf_layout` is chosen for heading recovery.
  - `pdfplumber` is worst.
- RQ3:
  - ModernBERT has the highest nDCG@10.
  - e5-small-v2 beats MiniLM and is the speed/quality compromise.
  - BGE is not significant.
- RQ4:
  - BM25 Only is below Dense.
  - Convex beats Dense with the *benchmark* BM25.
  - All dataset counts are right.
- RQ5:
  - The sample is clean within the project.
  - Table 5.6, Table 5.7, the 244-question figure, the correlations and the attribution correction all reproduce.
  - 3b > 1b on Token F1.

**Must be narrowed or withdrawn:**
1. **RQ4 "RRF (k=60) [Sản phẩm] ≈ Dense Only"** (abstract, §5.5, §5.7–5.9, Ch.6). This is unsupported.
   - The tested RRF arm used benchmark BM25, not production BM25.
   - With production-style BM25 (E1), RRF is significantly *below* dense: Δ −0.0426 [−0.0563; −0.0280].
   - The stated reason for keeping RRF (local IDF compression) is also not supported.
   - The fix is rewording using the E1 result, labelled as a post-selection diagnostic. No new experiment is needed.
2. **RQ2 "all strategies compared under the same 1,600-token budget".** This is unsupported.
   - nDCG, Recall and MRR are computed on the full ranked list, without the budget.
   - The only budget-aware metric is stored but not reported: coverage of evidence within 1,600 tokens (`context_budget_coverage_pct`). On it, hierarchical (81.1%) ties `fixed_size_overlap` (81.1%) and is close to `fixed_size` (78.7%).
   - The `hierarchical_child` "collapse" mostly reflects the scoring unit.
3. **RQ3 Pareto claim.** `arctic-embed-m-v1.5` is not on the frontier, because e5 beats it on both axes.
   - The independence caveat should also say that the 159 held-out papers were used earlier to choose e5.
4. **Wrong method details** (factual fixes):
   - RQ4 budget uses words × 1.33, not `cl100k_base`.
   - Relevance uses regex tokens, not WordPiece.
   - Long parents are cut to their first 400 words, not windowed.
   - Appendix E names `rank_bm25` for the benchmark (wrong) and gives Dev max questions per paper as 8 (actually 10).
   - §3.2 says the 777 Train papers are unused; 527 are, after RQ5.
5. Smaller overstatements ("chứng minh" from non-significant results; causal explanations that were not tested; Boolean F1 ranking) should be softened. RAM figures should be removed.

**Unresolved:**
- Which 2 RQ4 Dev questions have no relevant parent. R_q is not stored per query; they are among 95 queries that score 0 in every arm.
- Why RQ2's 0.688 drops to 0.505 in the production parity run (pending E2, if wanted).
- Whether QASPER full text is LaTeX-derived, as claimed in the Char-Match explanation.
- External pretraining contamination for any paper (cannot be established).

**Minimum corrective work:**
- Thesis rewording only, using existing artifacts including E1.
- Optionally, two append-only registry notes.
- **No new experiment is required** for the thesis to be defensible.
- E2 and E3 are optional: each repairs only claims that can instead be reworded (§9).

**Can be omitted:** E2, E3, E4 (RQ6-lite), E5, a second human reviewer, and new datasets. The reasons are in §10.

## 3. Validity findings per RQ

### RQ1 (PDF extraction; 47 papers, 127 q)
- **Cohort:** the in-house Dev papers with a local PDF over 10 kB (`evaluate_rq1_extractors.py:556-580`). One paper (`1802.00396`) was lost because its PDF is unavailable, so selection bias is negligible. The cohort is arXiv NLP papers only.
- **Extraction metrics:** they compare against QASPER JSON text. Heading F1 is implemented only for `pymupdf_layout`, so the other tools score 0 by construction; the thesis discloses this. Downstream nDCG uses e5 with τ = 0.55 (L706-710).
- **Downstream nDCG@10:** reproduced from the JSONL (0.5274 / 0.5100 / 0.5174 / 0.5131 / 0.3769). `pdfplumber` has 30/127 queries with no relevant chunk.
- **Interpretation:** "not significant" among the top four is not equivalence (C05). Extraction quality and retrieval quality are reported separately, which is correct.

### RQ2 (chunking; same 47 / 127)
- **Budget:** the 1,600-token budget is not applied to the reported metrics (`evaluate_rq2_chunking.py:130-152` for parents, `:181-195` for flat chunks). It only feeds `budget_covered` (`:154-161`, `:197-206`), and that loop includes the unit that crosses the budget.
- **Stored budget coverage:**

  | Strategy | Coverage within 1,600 tokens |
  |---|---|
  | `hierarchical` | 81.1% |
  | `fixed_size_overlap` | 81.1% |
  | `semantic` | 80.3% |
  | `section_token` | 80.3% |
  | `hierarchical_child` | 80.3% |
  | `fixed_size` | 78.7% |
  | `section_overlap` | 76.4% |
  | `section` | 74.8% |

- **Ground truth depends on the unit.** R_q is counted in each strategy's own units (mean R_q 2.2 for parents vs 3.4 for children). Containment favours larger units. 3 queries have no relevant unit under some strategies but do under others (RQ2-02). nDCG across different units is therefore not a like-for-like comparison. This is an invalid-protocol problem (insufficiency type 4), not a sample-size problem.
- **0.688 vs 0.505:** the cause is unresolved. Known differences are the budget, the 5-parent cap, parent windowing and Qdrant search. The production parity run (dense only) gives hierarchical +0.0306 nDCG@10 and 75.6% vs 73.2% budget coverage over `section_overlap`.

### RQ3 (embeddings; 207 papers, 615 q)
- **Cohort and inputs:**
  - The cohort is reconstructed by ID and matches the artifact (207 / 615).
  - Text is QASPER JSON (not PDF), chunked with `section_overlap` 350/70.
  - Prefixes come from the model table (`run_thesis_embedding_benchmark.py:73-191`).
  - Relevance is τ* = 0.55.
- **Same items for every model:** all models are scored on one query list. The artifact does not record per-model counts.
- **Selection reuse (category A, low):**
  - E5 had been compared with MiniLM/BGE on these same papers before.
  - The unified ranking is a development benchmark, not a confirmation of E5.
  - Weights are frozen and only a few candidates were compared, so the optimism is small. It still has to be disclosed (C18).
  - Models first tested in RQ3 (ModernBERT, the Arctic models, GTE, Nomic, MXBAI) are not affected.
- **Pareto statement:** wrong for `arctic-embed-m-v1.5` (C23).
- **Registry:** EXP-RQ3 lists 211 papers / 450 questions; 169 RQ3 questions are missing at question level (RQ3-03). Paper-level exposure is complete.

### RQ4 (hybrid retrieval; Dev 276 / 892, Test 408 / 1,310)
- **Counts:** reproduced by ID and by an independent recount of the inclusion rule ("≥ 1 non-unanswerable annotation with non-FLOAT text evidence") on the official files. The 5 Dev and 8 Test papers that were dropped all have text but no eligible question; the registry's "zero chunks" wording is wrong.
- **Two Dev questions with no relevant parent** are kept in the means with score 0 (R_q floored to 1). This is a defined convention, but their IDs are not recoverable from the stored outputs.
- **Benchmark ≠ production** (the core finding):

  | Aspect | Benchmark | Production |
  |---|---|---|
  | BM25 | in-script `WordPieceBM25` (L89-135), 39 stopwords, IDF over the whole split | `BM25Okapi` on `lower().split()`, per document |
  | Candidates | top-20 | top-50 |
  | Long parents | first 400 words | window around the matched child |

  So the RQ4 "RRF [Sản phẩm]" arm does not describe production (E1). E1 is already computed but used Test a second time, so its Test result is a post-selection diagnostic. Its Dev result is directionally consistent.
- **Method text errors:** tokenizer, budget counter and parent relevance (C34–C36, C39).
- **Scope:** retrieval metrics are reported as retrieval metrics. The thesis already says they do not measure answers (C40).

### RQ5 (generation; 250 papers / 500 q, gold evidence)
- **Manifest:** 500 unique questions, 2 per paper, all in official Train. Every item has non-float text evidence and a reference. Primary, reserve and residual sets are disjoint; the replacement log is empty (RQ5-01).
- **Generations:** each model has exactly the 500 manifest questions, with 0 errors (RQ5-02).
- **Generator only:** context is the gold evidence (`evaluate_rq5_llm_generation.py:633`); retrieval is not involved.
- **Metrics are distinct and the thesis keeps them apart:**
  - Token F1 is max over annotators.
  - Refusal is detected by substring match (it misses 13 paraphrased refusals).
  - The containment proxy is lexical.
- **Boolean items:** F1 against "Yes"/"No" is uninformative for verbose answers. Binary accuracy is computable (72/72 gold labels), but nothing in the thesis currently needs it (C44).
- **The 244-question comparison** is conditional on both models answering, so it is selection-affected (C45).
- **Human review:**
  - It is the stride-25 subset (items 1, 26, …, 476), and the attribution correction verifies three ways (§1, H11).
  - There are 20 questions per model and one reviewer, so it is exploratory. No agreement statistic is possible, and none is claimed.
- **Pilot:** the 16 hand-written questions on 4 famous non-QASPER papers are a harness smoke test. They should not support any thesis claim, and the thesis does not use them for one.

## 6. Leakage classification

**A. Within-project leakage**
1. **RQ3 / E5.** E5 was chosen against MiniLM/BGE on the in-house Dev 48 and the held-out 159, then ranked on the same 207 papers.
   - Severity: low. Weights are frozen and few candidates were compared.
   - Affects: C18, C19 and C24.
   - Fix: wording.
2. **RQ4 Test reuse by E1.** E1 added new arms and a switching rule after the RQ4 Test results were known.
   - Severity: moderate for a confirmatory claim, low for a diagnostic one.
   - Affects any thesis use of E1 (C30–C33).
   - Fix: label E1 as a post-selection diagnostic. A fresh sample (E5) is needed only to claim a confirmed production improvement.

**B. Shared infrastructure (acceptable once disclosed)**
- RQ1/RQ2/RQ3 Dev papers inside RQ4 Dev.
- τ calibrated on the RQ1/RQ2 papers.
- Pilot sample papers inside RQ4 Dev.

None of these touches RQ4 Test or RQ5.

**C. External pretraining contamination**
- All QASPER papers are public arXiv preprints, and the four pilot papers are among the most cited in the field. The LLMs and embedding models may have seen any of them.
- Repository checks (registry, overlap checker) only show that the project itself did not reuse them. They say nothing about pretraining.
- Status: **cannot be established either way.** C41 adds this sentence.
